# Evaluation & Model Benchmark Report

> **Project**: Resume ↔ JD Fit Analyzer (PRD-6)  
> **Model Requirement Notice**: **Full Gemini Model (`gemini-flash-latest`) is REQUIRED.**

---

## 📌 Important Model Choice Notice

During benchmark evaluation on the 15-pair Gold Dataset, a critical performance difference was identified between Gemini Flash models:

| Model | Must-Have Match Accuracy | Nice-To-Have Match Accuracy | Unsupported Claims Rate | Fit Score MAE (Python) | Benchmark Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`gemini-flash-latest`** *(Full Model)* | **≥ 80.00%** | **≥ 80.00%** | **3.28%** (≤ 10%) | **≤ 15.00** | **PASSED PRD TARGETS** |
| **`gemini-flash-lite-latest`** *(Lite Model)* | **41.07%** | **39.02%** | **33.82%** | **48.13** | ❌ FAILED (Drops short JD requirements) |

> **Production Requirement**: The agent pipeline **MUST use `gemini-flash-latest`** (or `gemini-2.0-flash`). Using `gemini-flash-lite-latest` causes requirement extraction drops on concise Job Descriptions (e.g. entry-level roles like `pair_13`), resulting in false missing skills and low match accuracy.
